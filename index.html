Why can't TapeMate put the files into WeTransfer for me?

WeTransfer doesn't let other websites or apps drop files onto its page. Only you can add them, by dragging them in or picking them. TapeMate does everything up to that point, so it's one drag or one tap.

    Library

    When you start a new audition, the one you've just done is saved here with its date. Nothing is deleted unless you delete it.

    Where are my recordings saved?

    Inside this browser, on this computer. They aren't uploaded anywhere and they aren't in your Downloads folder until you download them. They stay here until you delete them.

    • Clearing your browser's history with “Cookies and other site data” ticked erases them.
    • A private or incognito window, a different browser, or TapeMate opened from a web link instead of this file each has its own, separate library.
    • Files you've already downloaded are separate copies and are never touched.

    Read each line the way the other character would say it. Tap Stop (or press the space bar) as soon as you finish. TapeMate trims the silence and saves it in this browser, so you only do it once.
